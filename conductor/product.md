# Product Guide

## Vision
Space Rocket Idle is an engaging idle simulation where players manage a space program. Build rockets, expand spaceports, conduct research, and complete contracts to grow an interstellar economy.

## Target Audience
- Casual gamers who enjoy satisfying progression loops
- Space enthusiasts who appreciate simulation and management themes
- Players looking for a low-pressure experience with meaningful long-term progression

## Implemented Features (v1.0)

### Resource System
- **Money**: Primary currency for construction and expansion
- **Science**: Used for research unlocks, generated by science rockets and explosions
- **Fuel**: Consumed per rocket launch, produced by refineries
- **Cargo**: Generated by successful launches, used for orbital construction

### Three-Layer Navigation
- **Orbit**: Space station construction and management
- **Surface**: Spaceport operations and contract management (horizontal scroll between views)
- **Research Lab**: Technology tree with 139 unlockable nodes

### Rocket System
- Two rocket types: Cargo (money) and Science (science points)
- Fuel consumption per launch
- Explosion mechanic with 50% base chance
- Salvage system to recover science from explosions
- Visual animations for ready, launching, and exploded states

### Spaceport Infrastructure
- Multiple spaceports with rocket capacity slots
- Passive science and cargo generation per spaceport
- Fuel refineries for fuel production
- Responsive grid layout for many spaceports

### Research Tree
Five technology branches with 139 total nodes:
- **Propulsion**: 100 levels of Safety Protocols (explosion reduction)
- **Infrastructure**: 10 Modular Spaceports, 5 Assembly Optimizations, 5 Recovery Protocols
- **Commercial**: Profit multipliers, contract bonuses
- **Orbital**: Unlock features (science rockets, spaceports, refineries, clearing)
- **Control**: Automation systems (auto-build, auto-salvage)

### Contracts System
- 10 industrial partner companies with leveling system
- Four contract types: Logistics, Research, Commercial, Balanced
- Contract conditions: time limits, explosion limits
- Rewards: money, science, company experience
- Visual progress bars for contract completion

### Automation
- Auto-Build: Automatically constructs cargo rockets
- Auto-Salvage: Automatically clears explosions
- Configurable intervals affected by research multipliers
- Progress bar indicators showing countdown to next action

### Quality of Life
- Resource HUD with production rates
- Fleet status summary
- Auto-save every 30 seconds
- Persistent state via localStorage
- Developer console (Ctrl+`) for testing

## Future Considerations

### Potential Expansions
