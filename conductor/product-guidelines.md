# Product Guidelines

## Tone and Voice
- **Scientific and Industrial:** The game should communicate with a blend of professional scientific jargon and rugged, industrial pragmatism.
- **Mission-Focused:** Messaging should feel like official space agency communications—direct, clear, and focused on the mission at hand.
- **Rugged Aesthetic:** Descriptions should reflect a "used future" where technology is advanced but functional and hard-working.

## Visual and UI/UX Design
- **Balanced Information:** Strive for high information density where it matters (e.g., resource panels), but maintain visual simplicity through the use of collapsible menus and tabs for secondary statistics.
- **Tactile Interaction:** Every action—from building a rocket to clearing debris—must provide satisfying visual and audio feedback to reinforce the feeling of physical management.
- **Industrial Minimalist:** The UI should be clean and functional, using a palette that evokes high-tech machinery and industrial control rooms.

## Progression and Complexity
- **Layered Introduction:** New resources, spaceport modes, and advanced technologies are introduced one at a time, allowing the player to master one layer before the next is added.
- **From Manual to Strategic:** As the player advances, the game naturally shifts from manual interaction to high-level strategic oversight, emphasizing long-term planning over minute-to-minute clicking.
